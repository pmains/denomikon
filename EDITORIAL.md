# 1. Scope

Poliscopic covers public meetings of councils, boards, commissions,
authorities, and committees within Maricopa County.

We generally do not cover:

* Private-sector decisions
* State government
* Federal government

unless they directly intersect with a local public meeting.

---

# 2. Editorial Values

These values guide story selection and framing. They do not override facts.

## Housing

Housing supply is a public good. We are generally supportive of policies that
increase housing production, especially near jobs and transit.

## Growth

We support responsible growth that expands opportunity and pays for its
infrastructure.

## Transportation

We support transit, bicycle infrastructure, walkability, and mixed-use
development, and examine whether public decisions make those goals easier or
harder to achieve.

## Water and Natural Resources

Water is a central constraint on growth in Arizona. We pay particular attention
to conservation, groundwater, and long-term supply planning.

## Clean Energy

We are interested in local decisions that affect renewable energy adoption and
emissions reduction.

## Public Safety

Public safety is a core government function. We cover policy, staffing,
equipment, accountability, and funding.

## Fiscal Responsibility

We scrutinize public spending. We favor transparent procurement, competitive
contracting, and clear cost-benefit analysis. We question no-bid contracts,
poorly justified subsidies, and spending that lacks accountability.

## Open Government

Public decisions should be understandable, traceable, and accessible. We pay
attention when governments make participation or oversight difficult.

---

# 3. Newsworthiness Filter

Before drafting an article, confirm the item has at least one of:

* A public hearing
* A policy change
* A significant expenditure
* A decision with identifiable winners and losers
* A development that affects residents
* A broader public-interest question

If the item has no meaningful public impact, do not force an article.

---

# 4. Article Workflow

1. Draft from the agenda item.
2. Find additional sources (minimum 3).
3. Add context.
4. Verify every factual claim against its source.
5. Add inline links.
6. Review for clarity and readability.
7. Publish and create a social media draft.

---

# 5. Featured Images

Include a featured image whenever a suitable freely-licensed image exists.

## Preferred Sources (in order)

1. Gage Skidmore (CC BY-SA 4.0)
2. Wikimedia Commons
3. Flickr (CC BY or CC BY-SA only)
4. U.S. government sources
5. Official press kits with compatible licenses

## Never Use

* News publication photos (copyright violation)
* Screenshots of agendas or PDFs
* Generic stock photography (unless CC-licensed)

## AI-Generated Images

Permitted when no freely-licensed alternative exists, provided the image is
labeled as AI-generated in the `image_credit` field. If no suitable image
exists at all, publish without one.

## Metaphorical Images

When a literal image is unavailable, a metaphorical image is acceptable if it
represents the theme accurately, looks geographically plausible for Arizona,
and does not imply it depicts the actual subject. Literal images are always
preferred.
