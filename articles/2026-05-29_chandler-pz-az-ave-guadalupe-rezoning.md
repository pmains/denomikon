The Chandler Planning and Zoning Commission will take up a rezoning at the intersection of Arizona Avenue and Guadalupe Road on June 3, alongside a request to withdraw a development plan for the Arizona Commerce Center and use permits for two bars.

**Arizona Avenue and Guadalupe Road Rezoning**

The commission will hear a rezoning request and preliminary development plan for property at Arizona Avenue and Guadalupe Road, one of Chandler's busier intersections. Arizona Avenue (State Route 87) is a major north-south corridor linking Chandler with Gilbert to the south and Mesa to the north. The area includes a mix of commercial development, multi-family housing, and vacant land. The specific property and proposed use were not listed in the agenda summary.

**Arizona Commerce Center Withdrawal**

A development plan for the Arizona Commerce Center is on the agenda with a request to withdraw. Withdrawals before a hearing typically indicate that the applicant has decided not to pursue the project in its current form, often to revise plans or address concerns raised by city staff or the public. The commerce center is located in Chandler's Price Road Corridor, an area zoned for employment and industrial uses.

**Use Permits for Two Bars**

The commission will consider two use permits related to bars and entertainment venues.

A use permit and entertainment use permit are on the agenda for Val's Bar. Permission to operate as a bar or entertainment venue in Chandler requires a use permit from the Planning and Zoning Commission, which evaluates impacts on surrounding properties, parking, noise, and compatibility with the neighborhood.

Lola's Liquors is scheduled for an entertainment use permit only, suggesting the business is already permitted as a bar but is seeking approval for live music, DJs, or other entertainment.

The commission's study session earlier in the day includes the same items, plus a recognition of former Chair Rick Heumann.
